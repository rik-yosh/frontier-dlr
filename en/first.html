1st Symposium

The 1st Symposium on Frontiers of Data Science and Life Science Research was held at Sanjo Conference Hall, The University of Tokyo, on October 21 (Mon) – 22 (Tue), 2024. Leading researchers in mathematical sciences, information science, and life sciences presented their latest research findings.

Venue

Sanjo Conference Hall, The University of Tokyo

7-3-1 Hongo, Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo 113-0033, Japan
