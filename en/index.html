Overview

The life and biomedical sciences have made remarkable advances and are now at a turning point where further breakthroughs are expected through collaboration across disciplines. Data science, in particular, has the potential to transform these fields. To accelerate this research and translate its outcomes into societal applications, we need a forum for sharing and discussing a broad range of knowledge, from the latest theories to practical applications and implementation. This symposium explores cutting-edge mathematical and information science techniques, examining both their theoretical foundations and potential applications in depth. It also considers applications to diverse data in the life and biomedical sciences, challenges in implementation, and future prospects from multiple perspectives. Through this symposium, we aim to establish a shared research foundation across disciplines, foster new collaborative research, strengthen links between academia and industry, and accelerate the translation of research outcomes into societal applications.
